Data Deletion Instructions

Threads Leads

Threads Leads only stores public post data (post ID, text, author username, permalink, timestamp) in a local file on the operator's computer. To have your data deleted:

  1. Email esbol@bk.ru with the subject "Data deletion" and the link(s) to your post(s) or your Threads username.
  2. We delete all matching records within 7 days and confirm by email.

If you connected your own Threads account to the application, you can revoke its access at any time in Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions → Threads Leads → Remove. Revoking access deletes the stored access token on our side at the next run.
