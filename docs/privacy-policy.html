Privacy Policy

Threads Leads · Effective date: 9 October 2026

Who we are

Threads Leads is operated by Individual Entrepreneur Yesbol Konsbayev (Republic of Kazakhstan), referred to below as "we". Contact: esbol@bk.ru.

What the application does

Threads Leads is a tool used by its operator to find public Threads posts in which people are looking for software developers, DevOps engineers and similar IT contractors. It sends keyword searches to the official Threads API and displays the matching public posts to the operator.

Data we access

Through the Threads API we access:

  • Basic profile information of the operator's own Threads account (user ID and username), needed to authenticate.
  • Public Threads posts returned by keyword search: post ID, text, author username, permalink, timestamp and media type.

We do not access private posts, direct messages, follower lists, or any data of users who have not made their posts public.

How we use the data

  • To show the operator public posts that match hiring or contract-work keywords.
  • To avoid showing the same post twice (we keep post IDs and permalinks of posts already seen).
  • To forward a link to a matching post, together with its author's username and a short excerpt, to a private notification channel that belongs to the operator.

We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or automated decisions about any person. We do not post, reply, like or follow on anyone's behalf.

Storage and retention

Matched posts are stored in a local file on the operator's own computer. Records are kept for no longer than 90 days and are then deleted. Access tokens are stored locally and are never shared.

Data deletion

If you are the author of a public post and want it removed from our local records, email esbol@bk.ru with the post link. We will delete it within 7 days. See also our Data Deletion Instructions.

Meta platform

Our use of Threads data is subject to the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. Revoking the application's access in your Threads settings (Settings → Account → Website permissions) stops all further access.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
